# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- Decision status: Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded
- Implementation status: Not started | In progress | Done | Not applicable
- Date proposed: YYYY-MM-DD
- Date decided: YYYY-MM-DD or `—`
- Deciders: names and roles that own this decision
- Depends on: ADR numbers this decision builds on
- Supersedes / Superseded by: ADR numbers, or `—`
- Open inputs: product, legal, or provider facts still required, or `none`

## Context

What forces this decision now. Include the constraint that makes the choice
non-obvious: scale, tenancy, money, privacy, provider behavior, or team size.
State facts, not intentions.

## Decision

The chosen option in the imperative. Keep the specification (schemas, APIs,
algorithms) in later sections; this section is what a reader must remember.

## Alternatives considered

Every ADR must list the options that were genuinely available and why they lost.
An option rejected for a reason that could change later must say what would make
it win.
